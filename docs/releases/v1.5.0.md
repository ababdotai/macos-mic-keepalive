# Mic KeepAlive 1.5.0

A native control panel and optional whisper boost for voice-driven coding, with a fix for AirPods audio-mode changes that could interrupt BlackHole output.

## What's new

- A persistent native panel with physical microphones first, frequency-ranked device tiles, always-visible startup settings, and inline guidance. Controls stay open while you adjust them.
- Optional whisper boost: +6, +12 or +18 dB with peak compression, sent to BlackHole 2ch for use in your dictation app. No AI denoising, recordings or uploads.
- Clear linked controls: enabling boost enables keep-alive; disabling boost preserves keep-alive; disabling keep-alive stops both. A persistent reminder tells you to switch dictation back to a physical microphone after stopping boost.
- Updated app icon and menu bar indicators: a shield for healthy keep-alive and a bold plus for active boost, with fixed badge sizes and spacing.
- Simplified English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese guidance.

## AirPods / BlackHole fix

AirPods capture can leave the audio engine's client format at mono/24 kHz even after the output device is bound to stereo/48 kHz BlackHole. The app now reads the bound hardware format, starts physical capture before configuring enhancement, and repairs output routing without repeatedly restarting the Bluetooth microphone. It verifies the destination after starting the engine and handles format changes when rebuilding the graph.

## Install and use

Download the universal DMG, drag **Mic KeepAlive.app** into **Applications**, then open it and allow microphone access. Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later are supported. A ZIP alternative and SHA-256 checksums are included.

This app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks this trusted download, try opening it, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

**Whisper boost requires BlackHole 2ch, installed separately.** The panel links to its official installer. Select a physical mic in Mic KeepAlive and BlackHole 2ch in your dictation app. Switch dictation back to the physical mic after stopping boost. Recognition improvement depends on your setup and has not been benchmarked.

## Validation

- Existing recovery, linked-control, metering, enhancement and localization tests passed.
- Universal arm64/x86_64 build, signature and DMG checks passed.
- With AirPods capture active and the MacBook lid closed, BlackHole loopback passed at 16/24/48 kHz, including three output-route recovery cycles per rate, without changing system default input/output.
- The installed app remained in healthy enhancement state during the closed-lid AirPods check; the user also reported restored operation. Intel and older macOS versions were not hardware-tested in this release.
